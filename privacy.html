FinTracker privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

FinTracker is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer and Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): to find account statement emails and download their PDF attachments to the owner's own computer. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Sheets: to write the parsed figures into the owner's own spreadsheet.

What it does with data

  • Data is processed only on the owner's computer and stored only there and in the owner's own Google Sheet.
  • No data is sold, shared with or sent to any third party, and none is used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local files can be deleted from the owner's computer.
